Pitfall: EIA weekly retail price series are not uniform across products. The petroleum/pri/gnd route publishes state series (SCA, SCO, SFL, SMA, SMN, SNY, SOH, STX, SWA) for gasoline, but for diesel (EPD2D) apparently only California; other states return no rows. Mapping a diesel stop in Texas to STX silently yields no data, so the app falls back to the national gasoline price and calls EIA on every request.

Seen in goal rv-profile-fuel-history (2026-11-26): caught at plan review (attempt 3); live check with DEMO_KEY confirmed SCA+EPD2D (2026-11-09, 7.897) while STX/SNY+EPD2D queries timed out on the demo key, so the rest is unconfirmed. Fix: getEiaAreaForState(state, fuelType) maps diesel to the PADD area except CA.

How to avoid: confirm which area/product pairs exist before mapping (one data query per pair with a real key), and keep the area mapping fuel-type aware. The EIA DEMO_KEY is heavily rate-limited; data endpoints often time out while metadata/facet endpoints answer.
