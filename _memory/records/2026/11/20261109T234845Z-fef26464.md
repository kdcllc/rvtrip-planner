Goal branded-email (2026-11-09). Problem: the first test mail from the new rvtrip@moedim.dev sender landed in junk even though SPF and DKIM passed. The password-reset email was the classic phishing shape: plain text, subject "Reset your password", one link. The other emails were bare HTML fragments, and the domain had no DMARC record.

What changed:
- `api/src/shared/email.ts` now builds all 3 emails (reset, invite, access-request notification) through one private `renderEmail({heading, paragraphs, cta, footerNote})` helper. It returns `{html, plainText}`.
  - The HTML has a doctype, `lang="en"`, a viewport meta and a title, inline styles only, and a ≤600px container with an "RV Trip Planner" header.
  - Each email has one CTA button plus the raw URL, and a footer explaining why it was sent with a link to APP_BASE_URL.
  - There are no images, scripts, styles or links to external files. Every interpolated value is passed through escapeHtml.
- The reset subject is now "Reset your RV Trip Planner password". Exports, signatures and the retry mapping are unchanged.
- `email-dns-records.bicep` writes a conditional `_dmarc` TXT record (`dmarcPolicy`, `dmarcReportAddress`). `main.bicep` sets `emailDmarcPolicy` to 'none' and builds `rua` from the sender address. This renders exactly the value the user had already added by hand.
- `email.test.ts` grew from 9 to 17 tests. The api suite has 304 tests.

Remember: the goal-folder checkers (`email_check.mjs` bundles email.ts with esbuild, aliasing @azure/communication-email to a stub shared through globalThis) are a reusable pattern for asserting rendered email content without vitest.
