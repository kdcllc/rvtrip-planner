Pitfall: element.scrollIntoView({ block: 'nearest', inline: 'center' }) on an item inside a horizontal carousel also scrolls every scrollable ancestor, including the page, when the item is taller than or below the viewport. In this app that pushed the map off screen on every timeline click and on trip load.

Seen in goal timeline-map-link (2026-11-26), reported by the user after the timeline redesign made clicking bars a common action.

How to avoid: to move a carousel, scroll the carousel element itself (container.scrollTo({ left }) computed from getBoundingClientRect), as src/features/trip/carouselScroll.ts does. Keep scrollIntoView for explicit "take me to this card" actions only. When a programmatic scroll is followed by a scroll-driven "current item" listener, suppress and cancel that listener's debounce until scrollend (with a timeout fallback), or the selection flips to whichever item ends up most centred.
