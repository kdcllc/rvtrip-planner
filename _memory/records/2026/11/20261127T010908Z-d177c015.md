Goal stop-weather-strip — outcome: shipped to PR #32 (branch feat/timeline-gantt-display, 2026-11-26), third goal on that branch after timeline-gantt-display and timeline-map-link.

Problem: the user disliked how the stop cards under the timeline show weather: one full-width row per day in a 220-320px card wrapped onto two lines, an "avg" chip repeated under every row, long stays made cards several times taller, and stay and weather were two separate boxes.

What changed: src/features/weather/StopWeatherForecast.tsx now renders a header (title, one-line summary such as "60–92°F · up to 73% rain", short NOAA link) and a horizontal strip of compact day tiles (weekday, date, icon, high, low, rain %) that scrolls sideways inside the card. Each tile has a visually hidden sentence for screen readers and an aria-hidden visual wrapper; the strip is focusable. Historical averages are stated once in a note (dashed tiles when mixed). Styles live in the new StopWeatherForecast.css; App.css makes .stop-item the single bordered card with the weather pinned to the bottom, and the current-stop highlight ring moved to .stop-item:has(> .stop-card--hovered).

Key decisions:
- Day strip inside the existing carousel cards rather than a selected-stop detail panel (which would need new homes for multi-select and reordering).
- TripForm.tsx untouched: the card unification is CSS only.

Remember next time: when a border moves from a child to a wrapper with overflow hidden, any ring or box-shadow on the child gets clipped; move it with :has(). WeatherIcon takes no aria-hidden prop, so hide it through a wrapper. Open follow-ups: the highlight ring is clipped at the top edge by .stop-list (pre-existing); Safari/VoiceOver drops list semantics with list-style none.
